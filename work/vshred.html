VShred: one ad, dozens of hooks.

Paid social burns through creative fast. For VShred on Meta and YouTube, ads were built as interchangeable modules — new hooks, intros and angles every week, without starting from scratch.

ClientVShred
AgencyKendago
RoleSenior Video Editor
PlatformsMeta · YouTube
Years2023 — 2026
The challenge

Winning ads fatigue fast.

Direct-response ads live in their first few seconds, and even winners wear out. The team needs a steady flow of fresh variations.

New footage for every test isn't realistic, so every edit has to be easy to remix.

My approach

Build in modules, test in batches.

Hook+Intro+Body+CTA

The body — offer and proof — stays. The hook and intro change. One strong body carries many openings.

Tests on hooks, angles, offers and CTAs, guided by CPA, ROAS and conversion rate, alongside PPC managers and creative leads.
