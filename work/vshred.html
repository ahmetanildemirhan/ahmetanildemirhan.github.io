VShred: one ad, dozens of hooks.

Paid social burns through creative fast. For VShred's quiz-funnel campaigns on Meta and YouTube, I built ads as interchangeable modules, so the team could test new hooks, intros and angles every week without rebuilding an ad from scratch.

ClientVShred
AgencyKendago
RoleSenior Video Editor
PlatformsMeta · YouTube
Years2023 — 2026
The challenge

Winning ads fatigue fast.

Direct-response campaigns live and die by their first few seconds. Even a winning ad loses steam once the same audience has seen it enough times, and the media buyers need a steady flow of fresh variations to keep costs down.

Shooting new footage for every test isn't realistic. The job was to find new angles quickly, and to make every edit easy to remix.

My approach

Build in modules, test in batches.

Hook+Intro+Body+CTA

Each ad is cut as separate blocks. The body — the offer and the proof — stays stable, while the hook and intro are swapped to test new angles. One strong body can carry many openings.

Working with PPC managers and creative leads, I ran structured tests on hooks, angles, core offers and CTAs, then used CPA, ROAS and conversion rates to decide what to iterate on next.
